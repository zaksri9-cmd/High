Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you accept crypto?

Crypto payment can be agreed for a specific order. The coin, network, amount and wallet address must be confirmed directly before payment. Network fees are paid by the sender unless otherwise agreed.

Can you guarantee same-day delivery?

No. Delivery depends on text length and complexity. An exact delivery time is confirmed in the quote before work begins.

Is the translation certified?

No. This is an independent language service, not certified or sworn translation. Legal, medical and similarly high-stakes documents are not accepted.

Will you keep my text private?

Your text is used to fulfil the agreed work and should not be published as a sample without your permission. Please avoid sending sensitive personal data that is not needed for the job.
