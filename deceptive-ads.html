How to Recognize Deceptive Ads and Unsafe Redirects

Advertising supports many free sites, but ads should never impersonate browser warnings or force you away from the page. Here is how to identify suspicious behavior.

Afterglow Editorial•5 min read•Adults 18+

Advertising supports many free sites, but ads should never impersonate browser warnings or force you away from the page. Here is how to identify suspicious behavior.

A legitimate ad should be distinguishable

Advertising should be identifiable as advertising and should not imitate operating-system messages, security alerts, or website controls. Be skeptical of designs that claim your phone is infected, that you have won a prize, or that you must immediately install an app to continue. A page cannot reliably diagnose your device just by displaying a web banner.

Watch for unexpected behavior

A new tab that opens without a clear action, repeated redirects, a fake close button, or a page that tries to prevent normal navigation should be treated cautiously. Do not click through to “fix” the problem. Close the tab using the browser’s normal controls. If it will not close, use the browser task switcher or restart the browser rather than interacting with the warning.

Never install a surprise download

Do not download APK files, configuration profiles, browser extensions, or desktop programs just to view content. If you need an app, look for it through the device’s official app store or the software publisher’s verified site. Check the publisher name, requested permissions, and reviews, but remember that these signals are not guarantees.

If a suspicious ad appears

Leave the page, do not provide credentials or payment data, and remove any file you downloaded but did not intentionally request. Run the security tools already supplied with your operating system and update your browser. If you entered a password, change it from a trusted page and enable two-factor authentication. If a payment was made, contact the payment provider promptly.

Advertising should respect the visitor

Good ad placement preserves the reading area, does not obscure navigation, and does not use forced redirects or deceptive controls. Publishers should test ads on mobile devices, limit intrusive formats, and provide a simple way to report harmful creatives.
