Understanding Digital Privacy

Your digital privacy matters whenever you browse the web, use an application, or share information online. Understanding how websites collect and use data can help you make more informed decisions.

What Is Digital Privacy?

Digital privacy concerns how your personal information is collected, stored, shared, and protected by websites, applications, and online services.

Build Safer Browsing Habits

Use strong, unique passwords, enable two-factor authentication when available, keep your browser updated, and review the permissions you grant to websites and applications.

Think Before You Share

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information with services you do not trust. Review privacy policies and check website addresses before entering payment details or account credentials.

Take Control of Your Online Experience

Digital safety is an ongoing process. Small, consistent privacy-conscious choices can help reduce unnecessary exposure and improve your awareness of online risks.
