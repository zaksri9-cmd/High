Copyright, DSA & Takedown Policy

We aim to respond to credible copyright, privacy and safety complaints. This page needs a working contact address before publication.

How to send a report

Send a notice to the real email address shown on our Contact page. Include the exact URL at issue, a description of the material, the right or concern involved, and enough information for us to understand and review the report. Do not attach or redistribute illegal or non-consensual imagery; a URL and description are usually enough to begin review.

Copyright notices

For a copyright complaint, identify the protected work and the allegedly infringing URL. Include your contact information, a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized, and a statement that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner. Any formal statutory requirements depend on the applicable law and should be checked before a notice is submitted.

Privacy and non-consensual material

Reports involving intimate images, personal data, consent, safety or suspected underage content are prioritized for review. We do not knowingly host or promote such material. Provide the URL and a safe way to reach you; do not send copies of the material unless a qualified authority specifically advises that it is necessary.

Review process

We will assess reports in context and may remove or restrict access to material, request further information, or take other appropriate steps. Submission of a notice does not guarantee a particular outcome. We may keep a limited record to document the complaint and our response.

EU Digital Services Act

If the service falls within the scope of the EU Digital Services Act or other local notice-and-action rules, the operator must implement the procedures and contact disclosures actually required for its circumstances. This simple email policy alone may not satisfy all requirements.

Before launch, add the real monitored email address and verify the notice-and-action requirements in every jurisdiction where you operate or target users.
