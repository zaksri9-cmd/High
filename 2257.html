This page is a compliance checklist, not a declaration that the site is compliant. Complete it truthfully based on the actual content and business model before submitting an adult site for review.

If you publish or host visual depictions of actual sexually explicit conduct, obtain qualified legal guidance about whether 18 U.S.C. §§ 2257 and 2257A apply and what records, statements and custodian details are required.

Site content model

At the time this starter template was created, Afterglow is designed as an editorial guide and does not include explicit media files. If you add explicit images, videos, creator uploads, embeds or links, reassess the legal and platform requirements before publication. A text statement alone does not establish compliance.

Required operator information

Where the law requires a records custodian or other designated person, publish the actual legal name and the required business address or other details exactly as advised by qualified counsel. Do not invent a custodian, address or exemption.

Content records and third-party material

Keep appropriate documentation for any material you publish or distribute and ensure all participants are adults, the material is lawful, and you have the necessary rights and permissions. Verify requirements for third-party uploads or embeds rather than assuming the original publisher’s statement covers your website.
